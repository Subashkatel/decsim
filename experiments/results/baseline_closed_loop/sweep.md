Reading the table. Windows are sliding (commit d, buffer d) and serial: window k+1 starts only after window k's boundary arrives, so the loop's capacity is one window per serial chain. Measured chain at the fastest input: 3.54 us per window = CWD transfer + decoder service + WDO delivery + boundary handoff, i.e. 0.85 rounds/us sustainable, a knee at a round period of about 1.18 us. Faster input only grows dep_block (the wait for the previous window). Decoder utilization at the LILLIPUT card is a few percent: with these link cards the baseline is link-bound, not decoder-bound.
